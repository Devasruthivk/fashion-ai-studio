What it detects

Three defects, one glance.

Structural damage

Holes, tears, cuts, and broken threads that weaken the weave.

Surface discoloration

Stains, fading, bleaching, and uneven dye across the surface.

Seam and edge fraying

Loose threads and unravelling along seams, hems, and edges.
